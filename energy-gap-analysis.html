Before you ask

What to expect
from the analysis.

  • $5,000
  • 4–6 weeks
  • Uses existing portfolio data
  • No new monitoring hardware required
01 Is an Energy Gap Analysis the same as a commercial energy audit?

No. Energy Gap Analysis compares buildings across your portfolio to identify where further investigation is most justified. A detailed commercial energy audit examines a specific building to identify causes and potential energy efficiency measures.

02 How much does an Energy Gap Analysis cost and how long does it take?

Rede’s Energy Gap Analysis costs $5,000 and typically takes four to six weeks. It uses existing utility and building information, so no new monitoring equipment needs to be installed.

03 What information do we need to provide?

Rede typically needs available utility bills, building type, floor area and basic operating information. The team will confirm exactly what is required before the analysis begins.

04 Will the analysis tell us exactly what is wrong with each building?

Not necessarily. Energy Gap Analysis identifies which buildings show higher-than-expected energy use or costs and where closer investigation may be worthwhile. A building energy audit, site investigation or recommissioning study may be recommended to determine the specific cause.

05 Can BC Hydro rebates, FortisBC rebates or SaskPower incentives help fund the next step?

Potentially. Rede can screen your buildings for relevant utility programs and help determine which recommended next steps may qualify. Rede is an approved service provider for BC Hydro’s Continuous Optimization Program, but final eligibility and funding are always confirmed by the applicable program provider.

06 Are we committed to another service after the Energy Gap Analysis?

No. You receive the findings, supporting numbers and a recommended next step. Your team can review the results, ask questions and decide whether or how to proceed.
