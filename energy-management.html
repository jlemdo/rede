Energy Management

Energy management is not something most organizations can do well off the corner of someone's desk.

It requires sustained attention, the right data, and the expertise to act on it. Most organizations do not have all three.

Talk to us about your situation Check your opportunity

The problem

What energy management actually means.

Active energy management is a continuous cycle. Without someone dedicated to running it, energy costs drift upward and capital decisions get made without the data to support them.

Rede runs that cycle for you, embedded in your organization, working alongside your team.

  1. 1 Identify opportunities
  2. 2 Develop strategies
  3. 3 Implement projects
  4. 4 Monitor outcomes
  5. and repeat

The partnership

We bring the expertise. You bring the buildings.

This is a genuine partnership. Rede brings the expertise, the platform, and the plan. Your team brings the buildings and the people who know them best.

One of the most valuable parts of every engagement is what we learn from your staff: the ongoing issues, the known problem buildings, the institutional knowledge that rarely makes it into a report. We surface that, work alongside your team, and build a program around what is actually happening in your facilities.

What the program includes

  • Structured onboarding
  • Target setting and baseline definition
  • Portfolio assessment
  • Energy project list and prioritization
  • Three-year capital project plan
  • Building automation scheduling
  • Regular team engagement
  • Carbon reporting
  • Incentive identification and applications

Every Energy Management engagement includes full access to RUN, Rede's utility management platform. Your data, your buildings, your plan, in one place.

What this looks like over time

34%

reduction in energy intensity, sustained year over year, with $4.4M saved over the course of the program.

One Western Canadian school division began working with Rede in 2016. At the start of the engagement, their portfolio of 22 facilities had an energy intensity of 302 ekWh per square metre annually. Today that figure is 198.

That kind of result does not come from a single project. It comes from a continuous program: data tracked monthly, buildings assessed annually, projects prioritized and implemented, and a team that stays engaged long enough to see the work compound.
