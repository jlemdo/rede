RUN, Rede Utility Navigator

Your utility data, organized and interpreted for the first time.

Most organizations have utility data scattered across vendors, spreadsheets, and departments. RUN brings it together, normalizes it, and gives you a clear picture of how your buildings are actually performing.

The platform

All your buildings. All your vendors. One place.

RUN consolidates utility data from all your vendors and buildings into one place. Every building, every meter, every vendor, tracked monthly and presented in a single view.

Utility data is collected and updated automatically. No manual entry, no chasing bills. Data is validated on ingestion and flagged when something looks off, so you can trust what you are seeing.

RUN portfolio summary

What RUN shows you

Six things you can see from day one.

  • Building Energy Performance Index

    How much energy each building uses per square metre, benchmarked against similar facilities. Tells you which buildings are underperforming and by how much, so you know where to focus first.

  • Weather normalization

    Energy consumption varies with the seasons. Weather normalization adjusts for that variation so you can see whether a building has actually improved, or just benefited from a mild winter.

  • Carbon and GHG tracking

    Emissions by building, by vendor, and by energy type. Tracked and reported with emission factors updated by province and vendor. Gives you what you need for board reporting without building it yourself.

  • Monthly cost and use trending

    Year-over-year monthly comparisons that show seasonal patterns and flag anomalies. Useful for catching problems early rather than discovering them at year end when the damage is done.

  • Portfolio benchmarking

    Your buildings ranked against each other by energy intensity and cost. A clear view of where the biggest opportunities sit across your portfolio, so capital and operational decisions are grounded in data.

  • Flexible exports

    Export visuals and data in the format that works for you. Clean slides for board presentations, a direct connection to Excel for custom analysis, and PDF for records. Whatever your audience needs, it is a single click away.
