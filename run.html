What RUN shows you

Monitor energy performance across your portfolio.

  • Building Energy Performance Index

    Compare how much energy each building uses per square metre. RUN’s Building Energy Performance Index helps you identify underperforming facilities and decide where to focus first.

  • Weather-normalized energy use

    Separate real performance changes from seasonal weather. Weather normalization helps show whether a building has improved or simply benefited from a milder winter.

  • Carbon and GHG tracking

    Track emissions by building, utility vendor and energy type. Emission factors are updated by province and vendor, giving your team consistent information for organizational and board reporting.

  • Monthly energy use and cost trends

    Compare monthly energy consumption and costs year over year. Identify unusual changes earlier instead of discovering them at the end of the reporting period.

  • Portfolio energy benchmarking

    Rank buildings by energy intensity and cost. See where the largest opportunities may exist across your portfolio and prioritize further investigation.

  • Reports and data exports

    Export available charts and data for presentations, further analysis and recordkeeping. Give facilities teams, leadership and boards the level of detail they need.

Before you ask

Questions
about RUN.

01 What is RUN?

RUN is an energy management platform for multi-site building portfolios. It brings together monthly utility data from your buildings and vendors so your team can compare performance, track costs and report from one place.

02 What can RUN show you?

RUN shows building energy performance, weather-normalized energy use, carbon and GHG information, monthly cost and use trends, and portfolio benchmarking.

03 Can RUN manage data from multiple buildings and utility vendors?

Yes. RUN consolidates utility data from multiple buildings, meters and vendors into one view, reducing the need to work across separate bills, portals and spreadsheets.

04 How often is the utility data updated?

Utility data is updated monthly, and facility information is updated annually.

05 What is the difference between RUN Intelligence and RUN Insights?

Both levels include access to the same platform. RUN Intelligence is intended for organizations with in-house expertise to interpret the data. RUN Insights adds two senior engineer sessions per year to help set a baseline, define targets and measure results.

06 Is RUN the same as an Energy Gap Analysis?

No. RUN is an ongoing subscription. The Energy Gap Analysis is a one-time analysis, completed using RUN, that identifies unnecessary energy costs and priority buildings before you commit to a subscription.
