The problem

Most organizations are not managing their energy. They are just paying for it.

Utility bills get processed, budgets get approved, and facilities teams stay busy putting out fires. But nobody is tracking whether the buildings are actually performing well, or what it would take to improve them. The result is unnecessary cost and emissions that compound quietly, year over year.

  • Teams stretched thin

    Energy management falls to whoever has time, which means it does not get done properly. Teams stay reactive, responding to problems instead of preventing them.

  • Data scattered everywhere

    Utility data lives in accounts payable and arrives from multiple vendors in different formats. Consolidating it takes real effort before it tells you anything useful.

  • Costs compound quietly

    Without benchmarking there is no way to know whether performance is good or bad. Budgets absorb the difference, and nobody sees where the money is going.

Rede closes that gap.

Before you ask

Straight answers.
Including the numbers.

01 Do all our buildings need a detailed energy audit?

Not necessarily. Rede’s Energy Gap Analysis compares energy use and cost across your portfolio to identify which buildings need attention and where a detailed energy audit is most likely to be worthwhile.

Rede can also check whether BC Hydro rebates, FortisBC rebates, SaskPower incentives, or other energy efficiency funding may be available before you decide how to proceed.

02 Is there a lower-cost alternative to conducting an energy audit on every building?

Yes. Rede’s Energy Gap Analysis costs $5,000 and typically takes four to six weeks.

It uses existing utility data to screen your portfolio before you invest in individual building energy audits, and helps clarify whether BC Hydro rebates, FortisBC rebates, SaskPower incentives, or other energy efficiency funding may support the recommended next steps.

03 What does an energy management system do for a multi-building portfolio?

An energy management system brings utility data from multiple buildings into one place so your team can compare performance, track energy costs, and identify changes over time.

RUN is Rede’s energy management system for organizations managing multiple buildings. It brings utility data into one portfolio view so your team can compare building performance, track energy use and costs, and identify unexpected changes earlier.

04 How can energy monitoring software help identify wasted energy?

Energy monitoring software reveals patterns that are difficult to see in individual utility bills.

Rede’s RUN organizes and weather-normalizes energy data so your team can compare buildings, detect energy drift, verify whether improvements are working, and prioritize the sites with the greatest opportunity.

05 Are BC Hydro rebates, FortisBC rebates, or other commercial energy rebates available for our buildings?

They may be. Available BC Hydro rebates, FortisBC rebates, and other commercial energy rebates depend on your location, utility provider, building type, and proposed work.

Rede can help identify relevant programs and determine whether your project may qualify.

06 Can SaskPower incentives help fund energy efficiency projects?

Potentially. Eligibility depends on the building type, location, energy use, proposed work, and current program requirements.

Rede can help Saskatchewan organizations identify relevant SaskPower incentives and determine whether a project may qualify before moving forward.

07 How do we apply for energy efficiency funding and incentives?

Rede can help identify energy efficiency funding and incentives that match your buildings and proposed improvements.

When a relevant program is available, the team can help you understand the eligibility requirements and application process.

08 What is building recommissioning, also known as retro commissioning?

Building recommissioning, sometimes called retro commissioning, improves how existing building systems operate without beginning with major equipment replacements.

Rede uses building automation system data and engineering analysis to uncover problems in schedules, controls, setpoints, and equipment sequences. The team then turns those findings into a prioritized action plan, works alongside your authorized controls contractor, and helps track whether the changes deliver results.

09 Can older commercial or institutional buildings improve energy efficiency without major equipment replacements?

Yes. Many commercial and institutional buildings can reduce energy waste by improving how their existing systems operate.

Rede first evaluates the building, available controls, and system information to determine which energy efficiency services are appropriate.

10 Do we need an energy manager to use energy management services?

No. Rede’s energy management services can provide the planning, analysis, and ongoing engineering support your organization needs.

The level of support can complement your existing team without requiring you to hire a full-time energy manager.

11 How can we reduce building energy costs without adding more work to our team?

Rede helps identify opportunities, organize energy data, maintain the action plan, and monitor results over time.

Your team remains informed and involved without having to build a complete internal energy management function.

Before you ask

Common questions.

10 answers

01What does the Energy Gap Analysis cost?

$5,000. It takes four to six weeks from data access to the presentation of findings.

We analyze one to two years of utility bills across your portfolio, benchmark each facility against its peers, and give you a specific figure for recoverable savings.

02Will Rede be making changes to our building systems?

No. Rede never makes changes to your building automation system directly.

All corrections are made by your own controls contractor, with your authorization. We find the drift and document exactly what needs to change. Your team decides what gets implemented.

03Do we need an energy manager on staff?

No.

RUN Intelligence assumes you have someone in-house who can interpret energy data. If you do not, RUN Insights adds two senior engineer sessions a year, and Energy Management puts Rede in that role, embedded alongside your team.

04How much of our team's time does this take?

Less than most people expect. Recommissioning is a desktop-based activity and requires no site visits.

What we need is remote access to your building automation system, your mechanical and electrical drawings, and a controls lead who can implement the findings.

05How long before we see savings?

Every recommissioning recommendation is selected for a two-year payback or less, sooner when incentives apply. RUN pays for itself faster: typical payback is two to five months.

Energy management is a longer horizon. One Western Canadian school division began working with Rede in 2016; that engagement has now accumulated $4.4M in savings.

06What if our buildings are older, or we have no modern controls?
