About Rede

We have been doing this since 2008.

Small by design, embedded by practice, and genuinely committed to the organizations we work with.

Who we are

Founded for communities that needed the work done well.

Rede was founded to serve Indigenous, Northern, and rural communities across Western Canada, places where resources were tight and no one else was doing the work. That origin shaped everything about how we operate: the relationships we build, the data we rely on, and the long-term view we take.

We have grown since then, and the organizations we work with have grown with us. But the approach has not changed. We are still a small, nimble firm. We still show up for the long term. We still believe the people operating the buildings are essential to any energy management program worth running.

We are engineers, but that is not all we are. We are project managers, data analysts, and long-term partners who work alongside your team rather than delivering a report and moving on.

Team on site with a client

What makes the difference

Three things that do not change.

  • Your data, your buildings, your plan

    We do not apply a generic program and hope it fits. Every engagement starts with what is actually happening in your portfolio.

  • Embedded for the long term

    Energy management requires sustained attention, and that means a partner who stays engaged for as long as the relationship is useful.

  • Data first, always

    Every recommendation we make is grounded in building performance data. If we cannot measure it, we will not claim it.

More than engineers.

Rede is a small team of engineers and energy efficiency professionals. We specialize in institutional facilities and complex building environments, and we care about the organizations and communities we work with.
